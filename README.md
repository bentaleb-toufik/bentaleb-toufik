I design **advanced controllers, state estimators and diagnostic algorithms** for robotics, automotive and industrial systems. My work turns mathematical models and data into embedded solutions, from requirements and simulation through implementation, integration and validation.

I hold a **PhD in Control Systems from the University of Siena (2015)** and bring **15+ years of research and engineering experience**. My background spans academic research and industrial development, connecting control theory, software and physical systems.

### Engineering expertise

| Area | What I work on |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Advanced controller design** | Robust, adaptive, optimal and nonlinear control; LPV methods; PID tuning; control allocation; actuator and operating constraints |
| **Dynamics & state estimation** | Dynamic modelling, system identification, observers, Kalman filtering, EKF/UKF and sensor fusion |
| **Intelligent diagnostics** | Model-based fault detection, machine learning, neural models, residual learning and configurable diagnostic algorithms |
| **Embedded development** | MATLAB/Simulink, C/C++, Python, STM32/STM8, CAN/LIN, algorithm integration and real-time implementation |
| **Robotics & simulation** | ROS 2, MuJoCo, trajectory optimization, robot dynamics and simulation-based controller development |
| **Systems engineering** | Requirements analysis, system architecture, traceability, ASPICE, verification and validation |

### Research and industrial experience

- **Automotive systems** — vehicle dynamics, steering and steer-by-wire research, ADAS-related control, actuation and embedded diagnostics.
- **Robotics and mechatronics** — dynamic modelling, estimation, optimization and advanced controller design.
- **Industrial engineering** — translating system requirements into algorithms, embedded software and testable behaviour.
- **Academic research** — experience with the University of Siena, FAU Erlangen, LAMIH and IFSTTAR, alongside industrial work at AKWEL.

### Engineering approach

**Define the problem → Model the dynamics → Design the controller → Simulate → Implement → Validate.**

I start with the physics, measurements and operating limits, then choose methods that can be tested and justified. I assess learning-based approaches against established engineering solutions and the resources available on the target hardware.
